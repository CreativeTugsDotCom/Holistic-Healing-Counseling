Crisis & Community Support Directory

If you or someone you love is experiencing a mental health emergency, please reach out to one of the immediate 24/7 resources below.

Emergency & Immediate Crisis

  • Emergency: Dial 911 or go to your nearest emergency room
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or Text 988 (or 1-800-273-TALK / 8255)
  • Crisis Text Line: Text "HOME" to 741-741
  • National Hopeline Network: 1-800-SUICIDE (1-800-784-2433)

Additional Urgent & Local Support

  • Self-Harm Hotline: 1-800-DONT CUT (1-800-366-8288)
  • Essential Local & Community Services: Dial 211 (211.org)
  • Veterans Crisis Line: Dial 988 then press 1 (veteranscrisisline.net)
  • Lifeline Online Chat: 988lifeline.org

Additional Specialized Support Hotlines

National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233

National Council on Alcoholism & Drug Dependency: 1-800-622-2255

National Crisis Line (Anorexia & Bulimia): 1-800-233-4357

TREVOR Crisis Hotline (LGBTQ+ Youth): 1-866-488-7386

TransLifeline: 1-877-565-8860 (translifeline.org)

LGBTQ+ Help Center: 1-888-843-4564

Planned Parenthood Hotline: 1-800-230-PLAN (7526)

AIDS Crisis Line: 1-800-221-7044

American Assoc. of Poison Control Centers: 1-800-222-1222
